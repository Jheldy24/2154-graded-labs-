# Week 4 grading guide

One D2L submission: **5% total**.

## Part A — guided challenge: 3%

- 0.75%: correct data loading, grayscale conversion, and split handling.
- 0.75%: reproducible baseline segmentation with a stated method and parameter.
- 0.75%: morphology operation is implemented, parameterized, and explained.
- 0.75%: outputs, measurements, visual evidence, and Part A report are complete.

## Part B — independent extension: 2%

- 0.75%: extension addresses a real observed problem and is reproducible.
- 0.75%: before/after evidence and trade-off are explained.
- 0.50%: limitations, AI declaration, tests, and final recommendation are complete.

Missing or inaccessible files can prevent the related criterion from being assessed. The two parts are marking components of one submission, not separate submissions.
