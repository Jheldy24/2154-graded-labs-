# AIDA 2154 Week 6 grading guide

One D2L submission: **5% total**.

## Part A — guided classification baseline: 3%

- 0.50%: correct data loading, label inspection, and fixed split preservation.
- 0.75%: feature matrix is reproducibly created and explained.
- 0.50%: majority-class baseline is implemented and reported.
- 0.75%: classical classifier and required metrics are implemented.
- 0.50%: confusion matrix, representative review, and Part A report are complete.

## Part B — independent improvement: 2%

- 0.75%: meaningful change addresses a question or problem from Part A.
- 0.50%: before/after comparison uses reproducible evidence.
- 0.50%: trade-off and final decision are explained.
- 0.25%: tests, limitations, and AI declaration are complete.

Missing or inaccessible files can prevent the related criterion from being assessed. Part A and Part B remain components of one submission.
