# AIDA 2154 Week 5 grading guide

One D2L submission: **5% total**.

## Part A — guided ORB/BFMatcher challenge: 3%

- 0.50%: correct image loading, grayscale conversion, and data documentation.
- 0.75%: ORB keypoints and binary descriptors are created and explained.
- 0.75%: BFMatcher with Hamming distance is implemented and documented.
- 0.50%: baseline and filtered visual evidence are saved and discussed.
- 0.50%: Part A report contains complete, reproducible results.

## Part B — independent extension: 2%

- 0.75%: a meaningful change addresses a problem observed in Part A.
- 0.50%: before/after evidence and comparison values are complete.
- 0.50%: trade-off and final decision are explained.
- 0.25%: tests, limitations, and AI declaration are complete.

Missing or inaccessible files can prevent the related criterion from being assessed. Part A and Part B remain components of one submission.
